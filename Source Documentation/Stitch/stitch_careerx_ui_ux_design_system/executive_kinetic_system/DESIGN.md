## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an operational standard for modern career simulation and technical mastery platforms. It bridges the structured, document-first clarity of Notion with the refined precision, kinetic responsiveness, and tight utility of Linear.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Architectural & Deliberate:** Layouts are grounded, structured, and legible, removing cognitive overhead so complex career roadmaps feel executable.
- **Academic Authority Meets Executive Craft:** Grounded warm canvases pair with deep indigo accents to inspire professional competence and focus.
- **Instrument-Grade Finish:** Subtle micro-borders, clean typography scales, and tactile micro-interactions make interface widgets feel calibrated like modern workstation software.

### Aesthetic Paradigm
The interface follows a Warm Minimalist System paired with High-Density Productivity cues. Canvases are never sterile gray or stark white; they utilize warm paper tones balanced by clean white cards, razor-thin structured borders, and domain-specific color accents for specialized technical tracks.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ergonomic hierarchy designed for sustained focus. Contrast is enforced through crisp ink tones layered over a natural warm baseline.

### Base Canvases & Neutrals
- **Canvas Base:** `#F7F5F0` — Warm organic background minimizing optical fatigue.
- **Surface Elevation:** `#FFFFFF` — High-clarity workspace tier for cards, sheets, and popovers.
- **Primary Ink:** `#1B1B26` — Deep obsidian for primary titles, values, and dominant interface text.
- **Secondary Ink:** `#5B5E70` — Slate tone for descriptions, metadata, and column headers.
- **Muted Ink:** `#8E92A4` — Neutral low-priority token for placeholders, keyboard hints, and disabled labels.
- **Structural Border:** `#E5E3DC` — Visible edge definition for cards and divider rules.
- **Subtle Border:** `#EFECE6` — Low-contrast internal table lines and nested panel boundaries.

### Interactive Primary
- **Default State:** `#29236F` — Authoritative deep indigo for primary actions, current milestones, and active navigational tabs.
- **Hover State:** `#1F1957` — Compressed deep shade for hover and pressed feedback.
- **Soft Tint:** `#EEEDF8` — 8% tint equivalent used for badge backings and active selection rows.

### Specialized Career Track Semantic Tints
Specific paths utilize calibrated quadrangles (Tint, Accent, Border, Text):
- **Path 1 (Full Stack Developer):** Tint `#F0EEFF`, Accent `#4A3AFF`, Border `#D8D2FB`, Text `#2A1E8F`.
- **Path 2 (AI/ML Engineer):** Tint `#EBF7F5`, Accent `#1E8E7E`, Border `#C8ECE6`, Text `#0E5E53`.
- **Path 3 (Data Engineer):** Tint `#FDF6EB`, Accent `#C4821E`, Border `#F5E3C4`, Text `#784E0B`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes systematic density, clear visual rhythm, and instant scanning. 

### Rhythmic Rules
- **Eyebrow Headers:** Always rendered in full uppercase with `letter-spacing: 0.06em` and weight 600. Used exclusively above major section titles, simulation modules, and status blocks.
- **Numerical Metric Displays:** Large progress stats and simulation score counters use `display` or `headline-lg` with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Body Hierarchy:** Primary body blocks use `body-md` (14px) for high-density simulation screens, while long-form narrative briefings use `body-lg` (16px).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an 8pt baseline rhythm with 4pt micro-nudges for dense dashboard widgets.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid. Left operational sidebar fixed at 240px or 280px. Main stage uses 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) margins. Content cards align along clean vertical channels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout. Sidebar collapses into an edge rail. Margins scale down to 24px (`1.5rem`).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column reflow. Stack multi-column career metrics vertically. Margins clamp to 16px (`1rem`), and gutters shrink to 12px (`0.75rem`).

### Structural Density Rules
- Component internal paddings match operational constraints: tight simulation panels use `space-lg` (16px), while full-size hero metric containers utilize `space-xl` (24px).
- Vertical stack separation strictly defaults to `space-md` (12px) or `space-lg` (16px) to avoid disjointed white space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids harsh drops or high-contrast drop-shadows. Surfaces float cleanly using ambient, light-diffused shadows tinted with warm neutral pigment (`#1B1B26` with low alpha levels) layered alongside razor-thin structural borders (`#E5E3DC`).

### Layer Structure
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F7F5F0`. Completely flat without elevation.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Panels):** Solid `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1px `#E5E3DC`.
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(27, 27, 38, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(27, 27, 38, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover Cards / Menus):** Solid `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1px `#E5E3DC`.
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(27, 27, 38, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(27, 27, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Slide-over Drawers):** Solid `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1px `#E5E3DC`.
  - Ambient Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 27, 38, 0.10), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 27, 38, 0.05)`.
  - Backing Scrim: `rgba(27, 27, 38, 0.40)` with a subtle backdrop filter blur of `4px`.

## Shapes

The interface balances precision and warmth through consistent corner radii.

### Shape Assignment Specifications
- **Cards, Simulation Modules, and Major Containers:** Configured to `18px` to `20px` (represented by `rounded-xl` in this design system) creating comfortable visual boundaries.
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, and Modals:** Strictly standardized at `12px` (represented by `rounded-md` / token-adjusted elements) to retain clean interactive firmness without feeling sharp.
- **Status Tags, Role Pills, and Avatars:** Fully rounded pill (`9999px`) for contextual badges, path chips, and interactive filters.
- **Nested Inner Items:** Sub-panels within cards must scale down their radii to `10px` or `12px` to ensure harmonious concentric curvature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#29236F`, text `#FFFFFF`, border none, radius `12px`. Height 40px (padding 0 16px). Font 14px Medium. Hover transitions to `#1F1957`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1B1B26`, border 1px solid `#E5E3DC`, radius `12px`. Hover transitions to `#F7F5F0`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#D9383A`, border 1px solid `#F5C6C7`. Hover transitions to `#FDF2F2`.

### Chips & Path Status Badges
- **Shape & Geometry:** Height 24px, radius `9999px`, padding horizontal 10px. Typography: 11px uppercase eyebrow or 12px medium label.
- **Full Stack Path Badge:** Background `#F0EEFF`, text `#2A1E8F`, border 1px solid `#D8D2FB`.
- **AI/ML Path Badge:** Background `#EBF7F5`, text `#0E5E53`, border 1px solid `#C8ECE6`.
- **Data Engineering Path Badge:** Background `#FDF6EB`, text `#784E0B`, border 1px solid `#F5E3C4`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, radius `12px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E5E3DC`. Text `#1B1B26`, placeholder `#8E92A4`. Focus state applies a 1px border `#29236F` and an outer ring `0 0 0 3px #EEEDF8`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px × 18px, border 1.5px solid `#E5E3DC`, background `#FFFFFF`. Selected state switches background to `#29236F` with an internal white icon.

### Cards & Simulation Panels
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E5E3DC`, corner radius `18px` or `20px`.
- Padding defaults to 20px or 24px.
- Internal dividers use 1px solid `#EFECE6`. Header blocks contain an uppercase eyebrow label above an 18px or 22px semibold heading.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Row items utilize minimal separators (`#EFECE6`), vertical padding of 12px, and soft hover states (`#F7F5F0` with a corner radius of `8px`).
- Monospaced or tabular metadata aligns flush right in `#5B5E70`.
